## Editorial constraints

- **No guaranteed outcomes:** Do not invent success rates, case results, court approvals, or guaranteed early termination. Investigative and consulting services support decisions and counsel; they do not control a court's decision.
- **Past security clearances:** Both biographies describe Top Secret clearances held during prior careers. Do not describe either investigator as currently holding an active clearance.
- **Agency founding year:** No reliable agency founding year was established. The FALI emblem's “Since 1994” wording is organization branding and does not establish this agency's founding date or the investigators' membership start dates.
- **Attorney support:** Present federal probation work as investigation and consulting, including support for counsel. Do not suggest that investigators independently provide legal representation or act as attorneys.
- **Testimonials and pricing:** No credible client testimonials, published fees, case metrics, or documented outcomes were identified. Do not fabricate them.
- **FAQ:** The [existing FAQ](https://www.earlyterminationllc.com/faq-1) contains generic template language. Avoid inheriting its response-time and pricing promises as verified operating commitments.
- **Affiliation logos:** FALI and INTELLENET imagery appears on the original homepage. Use only the original assets when appropriate; do not infer additional certifications, endorsements, or membership dates from the graphics alone.
- **Scope:** Do not expand the published South Florida service area into a blanket nationwide field-investigation claim. The background page does mention nationwide criminal-history searches for employers; that narrower service is distinct from nationwide in-person coverage.
